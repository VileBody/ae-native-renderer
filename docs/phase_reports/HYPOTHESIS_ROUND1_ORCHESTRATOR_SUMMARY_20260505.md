## Decisions

### `M10`

Accepted:

- Current centered integer-radius Box Blur is exact for `EFF_030`.
- Drop Shadow direction tuple for the observed `135/28` case is consistent with
  current native telemetry.

Needs new probe:

- Full angle sweep, fractional softness, shadow alpha source with translucent
  colored input, and final under-composite discriminator.

No orchestrator blocker.

### `M11`

Accepted:

- `GlowBasedOn` parser/instrumentation can distinguish absent `0001`, `0001=1`,
  and `0001=2`.
- Animated Glow parameter sampling in `EFF_070` is instrumented.

Rejected:

- Tuning radius/intensity/final blend from current final pixels.

Needs new probe:

- AE threshold-source intermediate for absent `0001`, `0001=1`, and `0001=2`.

Orchestrator decision:

- The M11 `ORCHESTRATOR_BLOCKER` is not a reason to reopen the M19 RGBA8 normal
  composite lock. Treat it as an M11 effect-local composite/wrapper blocker until
  a Glow threshold-source probe separates threshold, blur, intensity, and final
  composite.

### `M12`

Accepted:

- `0003/0004/0005/0008/0009` property-index mapping.
- Recovered Geometry2 matrix order and layer-space inverse sampling.
- Geometry2 participates in `STK_030` at the expected adjustment-stack slot.

Rejected:

- Treating payload `0008` as scale height or opacity.
- Reordering the matrix to tune `EFF_040`.

Needs new probe:

- Pixel-center convention, sampler/OOB footprint, and `0012` bicubic enum.

No orchestrator blocker.

### `M13`

Accepted:

- For current probed values: `0001=1` minimum, `0001=2` maximum, `0003=1`
  color/RGB, and integer radius mapping.

Rejected:

- Inverted operation enum for `0001=2`.
- Treating `0003=1` as alpha-only.
- Tuning Minimax from `STK_020` final pixels alone.

Needs new probe:

- `0001=3/4`, `0003=3..6`, `0004=2/3`, fractional radius, edge and
  `0005 Don't Shrink Edges`.

Orchestrator decision:

- `STK_020` residual attribution remains unowned. Do not assign it to M13 until
  M10 shadow/blur and effect-local composite evidence are isolated.

### `M14`

Accepted:

- Field-first sidecars are useful and stable enough for native baseline work:
  hashes, sample grid, resolved params, fixed16 slots, `FracAll`/`Frac1D`
  labels, lookup dimensions, sampler mode, edge policy, and OOB counts.
- `STK_030` localizes Turbulent after Geometry2/Posterize/Minimax with live
  downstream param time.

Rejected:

- Current sine turbulence as AE field parity.
- Any M14 tuning from final PNGs, especially `STK_030`.
- Adding candidate field formulas before an AE field discriminator exists.

Needs new probe / blocker:

- AE coordinate-field vector goldens or kernel extraction. Without one of those,
  M14 cannot safely distinguish field-generator error from sampler, geometry, or
  composite error.

## Guardrail Update

Round 1 confirms that `hypothesis-pack` is useful as a standard experiment
wrapper, but most candidates are still `instrumented`, not `accepted`. A future
candidate should use `--status isolated-improved`, `--status rejected`, or
`--status needs-new-probe` only after the corresponding module report names the
discriminator and evidence.
